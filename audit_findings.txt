Workbook audit and exploratory reanalysis

The supplied workbook has the exact SHA256 named in the manuscript. All 192
outcome formulas implement the specified rule, and all 192 cached classifications
match independent reconstruction from literal eligibility and gate data. The
172 common-eligible classifications all match. No normalization is required for
the literal compiled gate labels. The 42 blank gate cells belong to seven
ineligible rater-question records and remain blank.

All reported headline Conger and Fleiss estimates reproduce. Sorting exact
DOIs lexicographically, using PCG64 seed 20261014 and 20,000 cluster draws also
reproduces every reported bootstrap interval to six decimals. Limited draws
7079 and 9976 are undefined, as stated in the manuscript. This ordering must
be made explicit for full computational reproducibility.

The gate states in 04_GATE_EVIDENCE agree with 03_GATE_MATRIX. One rationale
prefix conflicts with its state: W02-H G05 in record A is Pass, whereas
04_GATE_EVIDENCE!L258 begins Unresolved. It is preserved and flagged.

All 258 analyst-reference gate states match the recorded rules: 138 unanimous
retentions, 63 three-vote modes, 22 Limited Pass/Not required ties, 34 other
mixed-state resolutions, and one T03-L G01 override. The derived reference
contains 18 Supported, 21 Not supported and four Unresolved cases; five mixed
eligibility cases remain unclassified. All 48 source-check fields say No and
both panel-member fields say Not convened. It is a derivative comparison.

The workbook has a one-to-one mapping between 24 Paper_IDs and 24 exact DOIs.
Among the 43 common-eligible questions there are 21 paired DOI clusters and
one singleton, T01-L (10.1007/s11356-018-2070-7). These identifiers do not verify
independent datasets or campaigns. External development/demonstration registers

No original Reviewer_A.xlsx, Reviewer_B.xlsx, Reviewer_C(1).xlsx or

NEW DESCRIPTIVE RESULTS

The type-only rule Stronger -> Not supported and Limited -> Supported matches
160/172 derived labels (93.0%). This diagnoses case-type structure, not accuracy.

42 of the 54 disputed Limited question-gate units (77.8%) involve only Pass
and Not required. Stronger has 9/66 such disputed units (13.6%). The four
records use Not required 45, 48, 48 and 4 times, respectively, out of 258.

Deleting one gate at a time changes 0, 2, 1, 1, 6 and 6 of the 172 outcomes for
G01-G06, respectively. Removing G06 changes six records across four Stronger
questions (five Not supported -> Supported and one Not supported -> Unresolved).
This occurs despite 20/21 Stronger questions having a common G06 Fail, because
other Fail gates often preserve Not supported. Thus the final category conceals
both gate disagreement and multiple decisive constraints. Gate omission is a
structural diagnostic, not an argument to delete necessary criteria.

Leave-one-DOI-out Conger kappa ranges from 0.813245 to 0.839387 Overall and from
0.072937 to 0.222222 in Limited. These source-omission analyses do not establish
out-of-sample performance or remedy purposive selection.
